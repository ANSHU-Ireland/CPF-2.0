# Product Vision, Mission, and Principles

## Vision

Every consequential "can this person do the job with AI?" decision in Europe is
made by an accountable human looking at transparent, role-relevant evidence —
not at a black-box score.

## Mission

CPF gives employers verifiable evidence of how candidates direct, verify, and
correct AI on real work — and gives candidates a fair, transparent, contestable
assessment experience — through a platform that can prove, at any moment, how
it behaves.

## The problem

Employers now expect AI fluency in knowledge roles, but interviews and
conventional tests reveal almost nothing about how someone actually works with
AI: whether they frame problems, catch hallucinations, verify claims, own their
output, and escalate risk. Meanwhile, "AI-detection" and surveillance-style
proctoring destroy candidate trust and create legal exposure. The scarce
capability is **calibrated judgement with AI**, and nobody can see it.

## The product primitive

Not a score. An **Evidence Profile**: a human-finalised package of anchored
criterion scores, per-dimension evidence bands, ledger claims linked to
captured work evidence, reviewer confidence and limitations, integrity context
(separate), and interview follow-up probes.

## Principles (binding on every module)

1. **Evidence over verdicts.** CPF helps a competent human understand what was
   observed, with what confidence, for which role context. It never declares
   hiring outcomes.
2. **Provide the AI, don't police it.** The approved AI assistant is inside the
   workspace; the working process is the signal.
3. **Humans decide; the system proves it.** Meaningful review is enforced by
   the domain model (finalisation gates), not by policy prose.
4. **Minimum necessary data.** Category-separated events, session-scoped
   signals only, no raw keystrokes, retention clocks per category.
5. **Transparency before capture.** Disclosure acknowledgement is the only
   door into an assessment.
6. **Integrity informs, never convicts.** Signals give reviewers context;
   nothing auto-rejects.
7. **Fairness is operational.** Accommodations first, anchors + calibration +
   adjudication, subgroup monitoring, "insufficient evidence ≠ negative finding."
8. **Boring, provable engineering.** Guardrails live in types, constraints,
   state machines, and tests.
9. **Honest status, always.** Roadmap ≠ reality in any document, demo, or
   investor claim.
10. **Compliance is a feature.** Evidence packs, audit chains, and rights
    workflows are product surfaces, not annexes.

## Target customers (initial)

- EU/EEA companies hiring junior→mid individual contributors in software
  engineering and digital marketing (wedge per workbook; final wedge decision
  A-02/A-03 with founders).
- Design partners willing to pay for pilot assessments and share 30/60/90-day
  outcome data.

## North-star outcome

Employer repeat usage driven by **decision confidence**: % of assessments whose
evidence profile the employer rates as decision-useful, alongside candidate
fairness perception ≥ target and zero unresolved integrity-misuse complaints.

Guardrails: candidate completion rate, challenge rate + resolution time,
reviewer minutes per assessment, subgroup completion/score-distribution
monitoring (lawful, aggregate).

## Non-goals (v1)

- No placement/staffing marketplace (pending A-02)
- No automated candidate ranking or shortlisting
- No surveillance proctoring, camera analysis, or emotion inference — ever
  without explicit legal approval and product re-decision
- No general-purpose LMS in Phase 1–3 (Phase 4 scope)
- No fine-tuned proprietary models before task-level evaluation exists
